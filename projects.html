Bitcoin Pricing Model

I co-authored a paper published on SSRN that introduces a nonlinear optimization approach to price Bitcoin and other digital assets. SPX Options Strategy

In the Cornell Trading Competition, I designed a strategy using delta-neutral option structures like strangles and condors to trade S&P 500 index options. The strategy returned 17% in three months, with a Sharpe ratio of 2.54, and ranked in the top 10% of competitors.

A-H Arbitrage

During my time at CITIC Securities, I developed a mid-frequency statistical arbitrage strategy targeting dual-listed Chinese A and H shares. With a Sharpe ratio of 2.09, this strategy spanned 8 sectors and leveraged premium mispricing across exchanges.

PCA Stock Ranking

At Apex Capital Growth Advisors, I applied Principal Component Analysis to fundamental indicators, creating a composite score to rank SaaS companies based on adjusted cash flow. This technique helped our investment team identify scalable and resilient business models.
